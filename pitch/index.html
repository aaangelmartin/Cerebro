What did you learn?Technically, and about negotiation and marketplaces.
Technically

Measure by recomputing the game.

Price and delivery days are one package. A rail is only what it measures.

Negotiation

The human still opens the door.

Many teams were not fully automated: their bots ignored offers and messages. Deals started when we talked in person.

Marketplaces

Distribution, not only design.

Ours worked and few teams used it. The venue is part of the trade.

One more day

Take the market to the teams, not the teams to the market.
